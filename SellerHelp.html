Tips to Sell Faster

Small improvements add up—use these retail-proven practices to boost trust and speed.
1. Use clear, bright photos Upload multiple images from different angles with good lighting so buyers can trust the condition of your item.
2. Write detailed listings Include brand, size, condition, material, and any flaws. Accurate listings reduce questions and speed up sales.
3. Price competitively Search similar listings and price slightly below top results to attract faster buys.
4. Keep your inventory updated Remove sold items quickly and refresh active listings regularly so buyers see current stock.
5. Respond quickly Fast replies to buyer questions help build trust and close sales sooner.

Seller Rules

These rules help make sure every transaction is fair, safe, and professional.
Keep listings honest Only list items you own, and describe them accurately including condition and any defects.
Show the real item Upload photos of the actual product, not stock or promotional images.
Protect buyers Clearly state if there are tears, stains, missing parts, or other issues.
Honour the sale If a buyer agrees to purchase, complete the transaction promptly and update the listing once sold.
Keep prices transparent Avoid hidden fees or misleading offers; list total cost clearly.

Community Guidelines

We want Kloset Digs to be a trusted place for everyone. These guidelines keep the marketplace welcoming and fair.
Be respectful Treat buyers and other sellers with courtesy in all messages and listings.
No prohibited items Do not list items that are restricted, unsafe, or against platform rules.
Follow local law Make sure your listings comply with local commerce and resale regulations.
Keep communications clear Use polite, honest language in product details, pricing, and shipment or pickup instructions.
Support the community Give feedback, help new sellers, and keep Kloset Digs safe by reporting suspicious activity.
