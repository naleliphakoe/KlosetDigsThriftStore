Help Centre

Find answers to common questions and learn how to use Kloset Digs effectively.
How do I create an account? Click "Sign Up" and fill in your email, password, and profile details. Verify your email to activate your account and start buying or selling.
How do I list an item for sale? Go to "Create a Listing," upload photos, add a description, set your price, and submit. Your listing goes live immediately and appears in search results.
What payment methods do you accept? We accept credit/debit cards, PayPal, and bank transfers. All payments are processed securely through our payment partner.
How does shipping work? You can arrange shipping yourself or use our integrated partners. Buyers pay shipping at checkout. Provide tracking info to confirm delivery.
Can I edit or delete a listing? Yes, you can edit active listings anytime. To delete, mark the item as "Sold" or contact support if you need to remove it permanently.
What if there's a problem with my purchase? Contact the seller first. If unresolved, escalate to our support team with photos or evidence, and we'll help mediate a fair resolution.

Selling & Pricing Guide

Understand our fees, pricing strategies, and how to maximize your sales on Kloset Digs.
Listing Fees Creating a listing is free. You only pay when an item sells—our platform fee is 10% of the sale price.
Pricing Your Items Research similar listings to set competitive prices. Items priced 5–15% below average sell faster. Consider brand, condition, and demand.
Commission Breakdown For a R100 sale, you receive R90 after our 10% commission. Payment is processed within 3–5 business days after the buyer confirms delivery.
Bulk Discounts Sellers with 50+ active listings qualify for a reduced 8% commission. Contact support to confirm eligibility.
Bundle Deals Bundling similar items can attract buyers and increase average order value. Set a slight discount to encourage larger purchases.
Seasonal Trends Spring/summer drives demand for light clothing and dresses. Fall/winter boosts demand for coats and sweaters. Price accordingly to capitalize on trends.
