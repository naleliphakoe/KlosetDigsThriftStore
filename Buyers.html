How Buying Works

Kloset Digs connects buyers and sellers. We provide a safe platform and handle delivery logistics, while items are sold directly by individual sellers.
Browse and Purchase Explore listings from individual sellers and place your order securely through Kloset Digs.
Secure Payments Payments are processed securely through our platform to protect both buyers and sellers.
Order Confirmation Once payment is successful, the seller is notified to prepare the item for collection and delivery.
Tracking Your Order You will receive updates and tracking information once your order has been shipped.
Receiving Your Item Inspect your item upon delivery and report any issues promptly through our support channels.
Marketplace Responsibility Kloset Digs facilitates transactions and delivery logistics but does not own or manufacture the items listed by sellers.

Delivery & Shipping

We coordinate delivery between buyers and sellers using the shipping method selected during checkout.
Delivery Options Available delivery methods are shown during checkout. Shipping costs and estimated delivery times may vary depending on your location.
Logistics Coordination Kloset Digs arranges collection and delivery between buyers and sellers through trusted delivery partners.
Processing Time Sellers are expected to prepare orders promptly after purchase. Delivery times begin once the parcel has been handed over to the courier.
Order Tracking Tracking details are provided when available so you can monitor your parcel's progress.
Delivery Delays Unexpected delays due to weather, public holidays, or courier issues may affect delivery times. We will keep you informed whenever possible.
Incorrect Addresses Please ensure your shipping information is accurate. Kloset Digs cannot be held responsible for delays caused by incorrect addresses provided by users.

Refunds & Returns

Because items are sold by individual sellers, returns and refunds are handled on a case-by-case basis.
Change of Mind Returns due to a change of mind are generally not accepted unless agreed upon by the seller.
Items Not as Described If an item differs significantly from its listing description, buyers should contact support within 48 hours of receiving the order.
Damaged or Incorrect Items Please provide photos and details of the issue. We will work with both parties to reach a fair resolution.
Refund Eligibility Approved refunds will be processed to the original payment method after the matter has been reviewed.
Dispute Assistance Kloset Digs acts as a mediator between buyers and sellers and will assist in resolving disputes in good faith.
Non-Returnable Items Items that have been worn, altered, washed, or damaged after delivery may not qualify for a refund or return.
